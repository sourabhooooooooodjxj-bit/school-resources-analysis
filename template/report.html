💡 Importance of the System

The School Resource Analyzer provides a centralized way to collect, organize, and analyze important school resource information. It helps identify differences in the availability of teachers, classrooms, laboratories, computers, and toilet facilities among schools. By presenting the information in a clear and organized format, the system makes it easier to understand resource gaps and areas that may require attention. It can support better planning and help decision-makers consider the needs of individual schools when allocating resources. The system also reduces the difficulty of comparing information from different schools manually. Regular analysis of updated school data can help monitor changes in resources and support more effective educational planning and policy implementation.

✅ Conclusion

The School Resource Analyzer provides a clear view of school infrastructure and resource availability. By analyzing the collected data, resource gaps can be identified and schools requiring attention can be highlighted. This can support better planning, resource allocation, and educational policy implementation.
