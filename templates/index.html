About Our Project

This system collects and analyzes school data to identify infrastructure and resource gaps. Our project aims to improve the education system through better organization and analysis of school data. We collect information about schools, students, teachers, and available resources. This helps identify schools that may need additional support or resources. Our system provides useful recommendations based on the collected data. Through better planning and resource distribution, we aim to support equal and quality education for every student.

Problem Statement

Different schools may have differences in teachers, classrooms, laboratories and other educational resources.
Many schools face differences in the availability of teachers, students, infrastructure, and educational resources. Some schools may have a high number of students but fewer teachers, while others may not have enough resources to meet their needs. This imbalance can affect the quality of education and student learning. Our project aims to identify such problems by analyzing school data and providing recommendations. By using proper data and planning, educational resources can be distributed more effectively and fairly.
